The Central Hub (Spine & Sacroiliac Joint)

Located where the upper body meets the lower body, the sacroiliac (SI) joint and lumbar spine serve as the center of motion, shock absorption, and power transfer across your body's kinetic chain.

The Deep Local Stabilizing System

Transverse Abdominis, Multifidus, Rotatores, Pelvic Floor, Diaphragm

Function: These deep tissues form an internal cylinder of stability around your trunk. Rather than creating body movement, their primary job is to stop unwanted motion and brace the spine and SI joint against shearing, friction, torque, and excessive strain as your arms and legs move.

The Global Stabilizing System & Muscle Slings

Internal/External Obliques, Latissimus Dorsi, Gluteus Maximus, Hamstrings

Function: These outer muscle slings transfer loads between your extremities during functional movements, working in synchronization with the deep local core to provide stability and power transmission.
